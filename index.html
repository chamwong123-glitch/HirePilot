Mock interview

The interviewer asks each question aloud. Answer out loud as you would in the real interview. You'll get scores for content, structure, confidence and communication, plus a better version of your answer.

Tip: use headphones and a quiet room. Live transcription works best in Chrome or Edge. Aim for about 1–2 minutes per answer.
